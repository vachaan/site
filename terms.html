CLAUSE 11.1

About the Service Provider

VACHAAN is a digital solutions initiative offering website development, social media assistance, portfolio creation and student digital identity guidance.

The services are provided according to the agreed scope, pricing, deliverables and timelines. The applicable service provider or business entity details will be communicated as required for formal transactions.

CLAUSE 11.2

Official Ordering and Communication Channels

All service enquiries, order discussions and payment coordination must be initiated through the official Instagram account: @vachaan.co

The official email address, vachaanworks@gmail.com, will be used for:

  • Order confirmations.
  • Written service and pricing agreements.
  • Payment acknowledgements.
  • Delivery communications.
  • Customer support and complaints.
  • Other formal communications.

Customers should verify that they are communicating with the correct official account before sharing information or making payments.

CLAUSE 11.3

Service Scope and Pricing

Each service has a defined or mutually agreed scope.

The listed prices are indicative starting prices or package prices, as applicable. The final price may vary depending on the customer's requirements, complexity, additional features, workload and deliverables.

For services marked "Contact for Deal", the quotation will be provided after understanding the requirements.

No additional work outside the agreed scope will be assumed to be included in the original price.

CLAUSE 11.4

Order Placement and Confirmation

A customer may enquire about a service through the official Instagram account.

Before beginning work, VACHAAN and the customer should mutually agree upon:

  • The selected service.
  • The project requirements and scope.
  • The final price.
  • Expected deliverables.
  • Estimated delivery timeline.
  • Payment arrangements and any applicable milestones.

An order will be considered confirmed only after the agreed details have been communicated and a confirmation has been sent through the official email address.

VACHAAN reserves the right to decline requests that fall outside its service capabilities or that involve unlawful, misleading or inappropriate content.

CLAUSE 11.5

Payment Terms

Payment arrangements will be communicated and mutually agreed upon before work begins.

Depending on the project, payment may be requested in full or through agreed milestones.

Customers should make payments only through the official payment instructions communicated by VACHAAN. Payment references and transaction records should be retained for future verification.

Any additional charges arising from changes in requirements, extra features, third-party services or other agreed expenses will be communicated before they are incurred.

CLAUSE 11.6

Revisions and Additional Changes

For website creation and personal portfolio projects, a maximum of two revisions is included in the agreed package.

These revisions are intended for reasonable adjustments to the originally approved design and content.

The following may be treated as additional work:

  • A completely new design or layout after approval.
  • Additional pages or sections beyond the agreed scope.
  • New or complex functionality.
  • Significant changes to the original project requirements.
  • Repeated modifications beyond the included revision limit.

Any additional work and associated charges will be discussed and approved by the customer before implementation.

CLAUSE 11.7

Customer Responsibilities

Customers are expected to:

  • Provide accurate and complete information.
  • Supply content, images, logos and other required materials on time.
  • Ensure that submitted materials are owned, licensed or authorised for use.
  • Review drafts and provide feedback within a reasonable time.
  • Ensure that final content and information are correct before approval.
  • Maintain control over their own social media and other personal accounts.

Customers should never share sensitive information such as passwords, banking credentials or OTPs through ordinary messages. Where account access is genuinely required, an appropriate and secure method should be discussed.

VACHAAN is not responsible for errors caused by incorrect, incomplete or unauthorised information supplied by the customer.

CLAUSE 11.8

Delivery Timelines

Delivery timelines will be discussed and agreed upon before project commencement.

Actual completion may depend on:

  • Project complexity.
  • Timely submission of customer information.
  • Feedback and approvals.
  • Revision requests.
  • Availability of third-party platforms or services.

If a delay becomes reasonably foreseeable, the customer will be informed.

Delays caused by incomplete information, late feedback or third-party service interruptions may affect the original delivery estimate.

CLAUSE 11.9

Cancellation, Refunds and Disputes

If a customer wishes to cancel an order, they should contact VACHAAN through the official email address.

Refund eligibility will be assessed according to the stage of work, services already delivered, work completed, expenses incurred and the applicable legal requirements.

Where work has not commenced, the applicable refund position will be communicated after reviewing the payment and order arrangements.

Where work has already commenced or deliverables have been partially completed, the refund amount, if any, will be determined fairly in light of the work completed and applicable law.

Nothing in these Terms & Conditions is intended to remove or restrict any consumer rights available under applicable Indian law.

Any concerns or disputes should first be communicated through the official email address so that both parties have an opportunity to resolve the matter.

CLAUSE 11.10

Intellectual Property and Ownership

After the agreed payment obligations are fulfilled, customers will receive the deliverables specified in their confirmed order.

For personal and student portfolio website projects, the source code and basic setup instructions will be provided through Google Drive as part of the agreed service.

Customers retain ownership of their original content, personal information, logos and materials supplied by them.

VACHAAN retains ownership of its pre-existing reusable tools, methods, templates, components and development resources, unless otherwise agreed in writing.

Third-party images, fonts, software, libraries and other assets remain subject to their respective licences and usage conditions.

VACHAAN will not use customer projects, private information or completed work for promotional purposes or public portfolio display without appropriate permission.

Third-Party Platforms and Hosting

VACHAAN may use external platforms such as:

  • GitHub Pages.
  • Vercel.
  • Google Drive.
  • Instagram.
  • LinkedIn.

These platforms operate independently and are governed by their own terms, policies, pricing structures and availability.

Free hosting options, where applicable, are subject to the provider's terms, technical limitations and future policy changes.

Custom domain registration, renewal, hosting upgrades and configuration may require additional charges and are not automatically included in the listed package prices.

VACHAAN cannot guarantee uninterrupted availability or continued free access to any third-party platform.

CLAUSE 11.12 • PRIVACY INFORMATION

Privacy and Customer Information

Information shared by customers will be used for purposes reasonably connected with service enquiries, order processing, project execution, communication and support.

VACHAAN will take reasonable steps to protect customer information and avoid unnecessary disclosure.

Customer information will not knowingly be shared for unrelated promotional purposes without appropriate permission, except where disclosure is required by law or necessary for an agreed service.

Customers should avoid submitting sensitive personal or financial information unless it is genuinely necessary and an appropriate method of handling it has been agreed upon.

CLAUSE 11.13

Service Limitations and No Guarantee of Results

VACHAAN aims to provide services with care and professionalism. However, certain outcomes depend on factors outside its control.

For example:

  • Social media management does not guarantee followers, reach, engagement or sales.
  • Website creation does not guarantee search-engine rankings, traffic or business growth.
  • Portfolio creation does not guarantee employment, internships or professional opportunities.
  • Career guidance does not guarantee admission, placement, selection or recruitment.

VACHAAN will make reasonable efforts to deliver the agreed service but cannot promise outcomes determined by external platforms, audiences, employers, institutions or market conditions.

Changes to Services and Terms

VACHAAN may update its services, prices, packages and these Terms & Conditions as its operations develop.

Any updated terms will apply to new orders from the date they are published or communicated.

For an already confirmed order, the mutually agreed scope, price and conditions will remain applicable unless both parties agree to a change or an adjustment is required by law.

Legal Compliance

Customers must ensure that the materials, content and instructions they provide do not infringe intellectual property rights or violate applicable law.

Nothing in these Terms & Conditions limits rights or remedies that cannot legally be excluded under applicable law.
