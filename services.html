SERVICE D • SPECIAL STUDENT INITIATIVE

Personal / Student Portfolio Website

A personal portfolio is an effective way to present an individual's education, skills, projects, achievements, experience and professional interests in one place. VACHAAN helps students and professionals create personalised portfolio websites that reflect their identity and present their work in a structured and professional manner.

Personal introduction and professional summary
Education, coursework and academic background
Skills, technical knowledge and domain interests
Projects, repository links, case studies and practical work
Achievements, certifications, honours and co-curricular experience
Contact channels, resume download, and professional social links
Customised design based on the selected package
Delivery & Hosting Details:
The completed portfolio's source code and basic setup instructions will be shared securely through Google Drive. Where suitable, the website can be hosted using GitHub Pages or Vercel, subject to their respective terms and limitations. Custom domain registration, purchase, renewal and configuration are not included in the basic package and may involve additional costs.
