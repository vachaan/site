Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before initiating a project with VACHAAN.

How quickly do you respond?

We review every Google Form entry and respond via official email within 24 hours. For real-time updates, you can also DM @vachaan.co.

How is code delivered?

For student and business websites, complete source code and setup instructions are shared securely through Google Drive upon milestone completion.

Are revisions included?

Yes. Two reasonable design and content revisions are included in all standard packages to ensure your expectations are fulfilled.

How do payments work?

Payment instructions are communicated in writing before work begins. We support milestone-based agreements with verified payment receipts.
